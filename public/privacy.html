Privacy Policy

Effective 16 August 2026. This is the privacy policy of GH Fares, not of Meta, WhatsApp, or Facebook.

GH Fares is a transport information service for Ghana. People use it by messaging us on WhatsApp or Messenger. There is no GH Fares account, password, or consumer website chat. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can ask us to delete it.

Who we are

GH Fares operates the Meta app fares-reporting and the associated WhatsApp Business and Facebook Page experiences. We process messages so we can answer questions about fares, stations, fuel prices, queues, and road conditions.

What data we collect

We only process data you send us in the chat, plus what Meta must include so we can reply:

  • Identifiers. Your WhatsApp phone number, or your Messenger Page-scoped ID (PSID). We store a one-way hash of that identifier, not a public profile.
  • Messages. Text you type, menu taps, ice-breaker and button payloads, and slash commands (for example hi or kaneshie to bubuashie).
  • Location. Approximate coordinates, only if you choose to share your location in the chat so we can find a nearby station. We do not track you in the background.
  • Preferences. Add-ons you switch on (fuel watch, road alerts, saved route, queue watch, fare revisions, quick report), plus a saved station or daily route if you set one.
  • Reports you volunteer. Fare amounts, queue status, or road confirmations you send. We publish counts, never a driver name, vehicle, or number plate.
  • Technical delivery data. Message IDs and timestamps Meta sends with the webhook so we can answer in the same thread.
  • Landing page. If you open our chooser page, we may record an aggregate count of WhatsApp vs Messenger taps. That log is not linked to your chat identity. The page may store your channel choice in your browser’s local storage if you ask it to remember.

We do not use Facebook Login. We do not read your friends list, photos, timeline, contacts, or payment card details. We do not sell personal data.

How we use it

  • To understand your question and send a reply in WhatsApp or Messenger.
  • To remember add-ons, a station, or a route so later messages can be shorter.
  • To update shared public transport information (for example a fare report or a road confirmation) as anonymous counts.
  • To operate, secure, and debug the service, including webhook verification with Meta.

Legal bases we rely on: your request to provide the service (you messaged us), and legitimate interests in running a public transport index. Where a location share or add-on is optional, we process it because you chose to send it.

Who we share it with

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (WhatsApp and Messenger). Meta delivers your message to us and delivers our reply to you. Their terms and privacy policies apply to those apps. We do not control Meta’s processing.
  • Hosting. The service runs on Vercel. Webhook traffic is processed on their infrastructure in order to send a reply.

We do not share your identifier with advertisers or data brokers. Public replies and the HTTP API expose transport facts (fares, stations, incidents), not who asked.

How long we keep it

Preference records (add-ons, station, route) are kept so the assistant can continue the conversation. They are held in the running service and are removed when you ask us to delete them, when you send STOP and we clear alerts, or when the service is reset. We do not build a long-term marketing profile.

How to delete your data

You can request deletion at any time, at no cost, from any region. We will delete the subscriber record we hold for your chat (hashed identifier, add-ons, saved station and route, pending report context).

  1. Open the same GH Fares WhatsApp or Messenger thread.
  2. Send the exact words: DELETE MY DATA.
  3. We will confirm in the chat. That removes our copy of your subscriber record.

You can also send STOP to turn off alerts. Meta may still keep a copy of the message history inside WhatsApp or Messenger; that is controlled by Meta, not by GH Fares. Deleting the conversation in your phone app does not by itself erase our hashed subscriber record — send DELETE MY DATA as well.

If the chat is unavailable, open the GH Fares chooser and start a new thread, then send DELETE MY DATA. We cannot look up a person by Facebook name or email because we do not store those fields.

Children

GH Fares is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used the service, send DELETE MY DATA from that thread.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012, and similar laws), you may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold, or to object to processing. Use DELETE MY DATA in the chat for deletion. For other requests, send a plain-language message in the same thread (for example “what data do you have on me?”) and we will reply with what that hashed record contains: add-ons, station, and route — not a download of Meta’s message history.

Security

The service is served over HTTPS. Meta signs webhook traffic. Identifiers are hashed before we store a subscriber record. No method of transmission is completely secure; we take reasonable steps to limit what we keep.

Changes

If we change this policy in a material way, we will update this page and the effective date. Continued use of the chat after an update means the new policy applies to that use.

Contact

Privacy questions: message GH Fares on WhatsApp or Messenger, or open ghfares-whatsapp-mvp.vercel.app and start a chat. Data deletion: send DELETE MY DATA as described above.
