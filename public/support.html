Support.

Help, your data, and how to pass GH Fares on. The product is the chat. This page is the rest.

Get help

Open the WhatsApp thread and write in your own words. There is no ticket queue and no call centre.

  • A fare — send the route, for example accra to kumasi.
  • A problem with a reply — send hi, then describe what went wrong.
  • Add-ons — send MY ADDONS, or ADD FUEL, ADD ROADS.

Open WhatsApp

Your data

We store a hash of your chat id, add-ons you switched on, and reports you volunteer as anonymous counts.

  • Stop alerts: send STOP.
  • Erase our copy of your record: send DELETE MY DATA in the same thread.
  • Policy: Privacy.

Institutions

GPRTU and the MTTD receive the ledger as counts: chart, reported average, number of reports, dated. Partnership is a digest, not a rider website. Ask for it in WhatsApp from an official number, or start at the chooser.

What we will not do here

We will not take a fare report in a web form. We will not open WhatsApp or Messenger inside the browser. Support that needs a number belongs in the phone app, where the conversation already lives.
