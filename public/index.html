Ask like you text a friend

Short is fine. Pidgin is fine. If something is missing, it asks you — it never guesses a price.

  • “Kaneshie to Circle how much?” — the fare, or how to make the trip in two cars
  • “I dey go Madina” — it asks where you are starting from
  • “Where do I get car to Kasoa?” — the stations that load for Kasoa
  • “Does the Kasoa car pass Sakaman?” — yes or no, with the stops
  • “Petrol price Tema” — pump prices, cheapest first
  • “Any wahala on the motorway?” — what riders have reported
  • “They charged me 15” — logs what you paid so the next rider knows

What GH Fares does

Every fare and price comes from recorded data, not from the chatbot’s imagination.

Trotro fares

Fares between Accra stations, from the Accra trotro field survey re-based to today’s prices — clearly marked as estimates until GPRTU publishes a chart we can load. Intercity coaches (VIP, STC) use the operators’ published prices.

Stops and stations

The stops on a route in order, whether a car passes your stop, and which station loads for where you are going.

What riders paid

Charged more than the fare? Say what you paid. It stays unverified until two other riders at that station agree. No driver is ever named.

Road conditions

Send a photo of a pothole, flood or crash and say where it is. Bad roads are published at ghfares.com/roads so others are not stranded.

Fuel prices

Pump prices in Accra and Tema, cheapest first, with riders confirming what the pump shows.

Alerts, only if you want them

Turn on fuel, road, queue or fare alerts with one message — and turn off alerts stops them all.
